What this board is—and isn’t

The displayed forecast and probabilities come from one named source, not an average of incompatible models. Cook, RCP and Inside Elections ratings, Polling Forecast metadata, and Polymarket prices are shown as separate comparisons. A missing rating or market means “not available”, never “no chance”.
