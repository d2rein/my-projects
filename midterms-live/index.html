What “live” means here

Open America republishes official state-election-office results, normally updating its election-night feed every minute. Its 2026 feed does not cover every state and does not call races. Uncovered seats are clearly marked; the page never turns missing data into zero votes or a result.

The forecast shown only as context comes from the final cached forecast board. Live results always take precedence once votes appear.
