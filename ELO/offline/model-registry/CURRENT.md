Quick policy: start with the displayed 2027 ELO tip; follow an active Market Swap; ignore a grey Market Swap; a yellow Player Alert overrides both but requires a manual freshness check; a green Player Consensus is the same reversal with stronger support.

If asked, “I have a yellow flag but the market is 80% the other way, what should I do?”, the registered `2027_v1.1.1` answer is: **follow the yellow Player Alert after confirming the published team list and market timestamp are current; the market marker is grey because the player signal supersedes it. Record a manual override if you choose the market instead.**

Grand-final home/away ordering is administrative. Version 1.1.1 applies zero home advantage and zero travel adjustment in a grand final.
