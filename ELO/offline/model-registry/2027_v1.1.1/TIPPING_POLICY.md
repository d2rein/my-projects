# Tipping policy — 2027_v1.1.1

1. Start with the displayed 2027 ELO tip.
2. Follow an active Market Swap when it opposes the model and is at least ten percentage points more confident.
3. Do not follow a grey Market Swap: a protected Lineup Adjustment or Player Impact signal has superseded it.
4. A yellow Player Alert overrides the displayed tip and any Market Swap, subject to a manual freshness check.
5. A green Player Consensus is the same reversal supported by Full, Core and Creation profiles and should be followed with higher confidence.

Grand-final home/away order is administrative only: Base ELO applies neither home advantage nor travel adjustment.
